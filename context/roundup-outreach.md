## Timing (read first)

A roundup is strongest when readers can **download and try cleanly** — which they can't until
notarization. So **now** = a *soft* pitch: "coming soon, here's early access to review, worth a
spot when you next update the list." It builds the relationship, can earn a "one to watch"
mention + a backlink, and drives interested traffic to the capture page — without burning the
full pitch on a not-yet-installable app. **Post-notarization**, the same contact becomes the
full "please include it, here's the clean download" pitch (variant below).

## Rules that make or break this

1. **Personalize or don't send.** These authors get blasted. One genuine, specific line about
   *their* article (a take you agree with) is the difference between a reply and the trash.
2. **Verifiable claims only** — this audience fact-checks. Use the vendor-checked numbers.
3. **Low ask.** "Worth a look / a spot when you update it?" not "please rank us."
4. **Tag your links** `?src=<site>` (e.g. `?src=xda`) so any resulting traffic attributes — but
   note authors often link the bare domain, so roundup traffic may land as `src=direct`. Don't
   over-rely on it; the mention itself is the win.
