Why it's different

See everything. Undo anything.

Both panes show where files come from and where they go. A staging area collects them before you act. Every move, copy, rename and trash is reversible: multi-level ⌘Z, with a toast that says exactly what was undone.

Small, native, private.

Native Swift, 2.7 MB to download. No telemetry, no account, no subscription: everything runs on your Mac. Free while in beta.

Rides the Mac, not against it.

Real Finder tags that round-trip both ways. Finder's own conventions for copy, paste, go-to-folder and Quick Look, so your muscle memory just transfers.
