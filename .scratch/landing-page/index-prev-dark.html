Why Diptychon.

Every action has a key.

Chorded gestures for power, plus Finder's own conventions for copy, paste, go-to-folder and Quick Look, so your muscle memory just transfers.

Undo anything.

Every move, copy, rename and trash is a reversible operation. Multi-level ⌘Z, with a toast that tells you exactly what was undone.

Real Finder tags.

Tags here are genuine Apple Finder tags, and they round-trip both ways. Diptychon rides the ecosystem instead of inventing a private one.

MVP · free beta

Getting it onto your Mac.

Native Swift, no telemetry, 1.4 MB to download and 5 MB installed. Diptychon isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. You do it once, then never again:

  1. Download, unzip, and move Diptychon to your Applications folder.
  2. Right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again in the dialog.
  3. If macOS still blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, Open Anyway.
