Two panels, one window. Drag nothing — send the selected file across with a keystroke.

⌥⌘→

Sends the selected file to the other panel. That's the whole idea.

Every action has a key.

Chorded gestures for power, plus Finder's own conventions for copy, paste, go-to-folder and Quick Look, so your muscle memory just transfers.

Undo anything.

Every move, copy, rename and trash is reversible. Multi-level ⌘Z, with a toast that says exactly what was undone.

Real Finder tags.

Genuine Apple Finder tags that round-trip both ways. Diptychon rides the ecosystem instead of inventing its own.

Getting it, and the first launch

  1. Download the 1.4 MB build and drag Diptychon to your Applications folder.
  2. It's a free beta, not yet notarized by Apple — on first open, right-click the app and choose Open to get past Gatekeeper.
  3. Point the two panels at any folders and start moving. Everything is undoable.
