Two folders, side by side. Select on the left, send it right with a single keystroke.

Built for people who'd rather not reach for the mouse.

Every action has a key

Chorded gestures for power, plus Finder's own conventions for copy, paste, go-to-folder and Quick Look, so your muscle memory just transfers. Send the selected file to the other panel with ⌥⌘→.

Undo anything

Every move, copy, rename and trash is reversible. Multi-level ⌘Z, with a toast that says exactly what was undone.

Real Finder tags

Genuine Apple Finder tags that round-trip both ways. Diptychon rides the ecosystem instead of inventing its own.

Getting it, and the first launch

Download the latest release, drag Diptychon to your Applications folder, and open it. Because the beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, macOS will ask you to confirm the first time — right-click the app and choose Open, then confirm.

Prefer to compile it yourself? The full source is on GitHub. It's native Swift with no dependencies to wrangle.
