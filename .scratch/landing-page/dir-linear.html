2.0  Stage

Collect from anywhere. Act once.

3.0  View

Table, brief, columns. A terminal in the same window.

⌘1 turns a panel into brief view, up to three columns of names. ⌘2 turns it into a column browser, one folder per column. ⌘J opens the built-in terminal.
