      What it actually does

      See everything.
      Undo anything.

      Both panels show source and destination at once. A staging area collects files before you commit. Undo is multi-level across move, copy, rename and trash.

      Small, native,
      on your machine.

      Native Swift, 2.7 MB to download and 9 MB installed. No account, no telemetry, no subscription — nothing about your files leaves the Mac.

      Rides the Mac,
      not against it.

      Real Finder tags that round-trip both ways. Finder's own chords for copy, paste, go-to-folder and Quick Look, so your muscle memory transfers unchanged.

      The rule that removes the guesswork

      The arrow is just
      which side is inactive.

      There is no "source" setting to remember. The panel you are in is the source; the other one is the destination. Add ⇧ and the file moves instead of copies.

      Before you download

      What it doesn't do.

      • Not plannedRemote drives. No SFTP, no WebDAV, no S3. If you mount servers all day, ForkLift 4 is the better tool and we say so on the comparison page.
      • Overwrites can't be undone. Every other operation is reversible; an overwrite isn't, and the app tells you before it happens rather than faking a rollback.
      • It's a beta. Signed and notarized by Apple, free while the beta lasts. The price afterwards isn't decided — what is decided: never recurring billing.
